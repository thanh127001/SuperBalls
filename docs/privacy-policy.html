Privacy Policy

Super Balls · Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Super Balls (the “Game”), published by Wanderer (the “Developer,” “we,” or “us”), handles information.

1. Information and Accounts

The Game is a casual puzzle game and does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other contact details to play.

In the version covered by this policy, the Developer does not intentionally collect personal information through in-game forms or accounts. This statement should be confirmed against all SDKs and services included in the published build. Your device and Google Play may process information under their own policies independently of the Developer.

The current version of the Game does not display advertisements and does not integrate Unity LevelPlay (ironSource). We may add advertising services in a future update. Before such an update is released, we will revise this policy to explain the relevant providers and their data practices, and update applicable Google Play disclosures.

If the published build includes any additional third-party SDKs, such as analytics, crash reporting, sign-in, or cloud services, their data handling must be described here before publication.

3. Game Progress and Local Storage

The Game may store settings or progress on your device for gameplay purposes. If this is the case, this information remains on your device unless a feature explicitly transfers it to a service. Please verify this description against the released version of the Game.

4. Data Sharing

We do not intentionally sell personal information collected directly through the Game. In the version described here, we do not use an advertising mediation service to share player data with advertising networks. This description will be updated if our integrations change.

5. Security, Retention and Deletion

We take reasonable steps to protect information within our control. If the Game stores data only on your device, you can generally remove locally stored game data through Android app settings or by uninstalling the Game, subject to device backup behavior. If you have questions about information we control or wish to request deletion, contact us using the email address below.

6. Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for the audience identified in its Google Play listing. We do not intentionally request personal contact information from players in the Game. Parents or guardians may contact us about privacy concerns.
